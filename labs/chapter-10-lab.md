# Chapter 10 Lab: Operating and Monitoring Production Data Workloads

**Estimated time:** 20 minutes

## Exercise

Design walkthrough: configure Cloud Monitoring alerting policies for a Dataflow pipeline's system lag and Pub/Sub subscription backlog, and design a disaster-recovery approach for a BigQuery dataset using snapshots and cross-region datasets

## Key takeaway

Operational questions test whether you monitor the right leading-indicator metrics and actually validate recovery procedures, not just schedule backups.
