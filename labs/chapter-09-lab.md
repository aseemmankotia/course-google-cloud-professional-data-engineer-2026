# Chapter 9 Lab: BI, Visualization, and Data Sharing

**Estimated time:** 15 minutes

## Exercise

Design walkthrough: choose between Looker Studio and full Looker for an enterprise with governed semantic-layer requirements, and design an Analytics Hub exchange to share a curated dataset with a partner organization without copying data

## Key takeaway

BI and sharing questions test whether you pick the governed, no-copy option (Looker/LookML, Analytics Hub) over ad-hoc exports or lightweight tools when governance or scale is described.
