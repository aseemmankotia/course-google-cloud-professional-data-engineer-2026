# Chapter 1 Lab: Choosing the Right Storage and Processing Architecture

**Estimated time:** 25 minutes

## Exercise

Design walkthrough: given 5 scenario requirements, map each to the correct GCP storage/processing service and justify a partitioning/clustering choice in BigQuery

## Key takeaway

Every storage/processing decision on the exam maps a specific requirement (consistency, scale, latency, cost) to one correct GCP service — memorize the decision matrix, not the definitions.
